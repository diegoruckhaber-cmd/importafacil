# Stage 66 — Customer onboarding in the simulator

Stage 66 reduces the learning curve for first-time users without changing the calculation contract.

## What changed

The simulator now includes:
- a short "Como preencher" guide before the form;
- plain-language help under the fields that most commonly generate doubt;
- explicit guidance for destination UF, exchange rate, freight, insurance, storage, target margin, transport mode and customs declaration;
- product-level guidance for NCM, country of origin, FOB value, net weight and destination of the goods;
- a reminder not to guess unknown data and to rely on the simulator's validation messages.

Advanced tax treatments remain collapsed and unchanged.

## Boundaries

This stage changes presentation and guidance only. It does not change:
- any tax formula;
- NCM resolution;
- federal or state rates;
- defense-commercial logic;
- saved snapshot structure;
- billing or entitlement;
- release scope.
