# Federal MVP implementation boundary

The federal layer is intentionally conservative.

- II is resolved from the statutory rate plus an explicit treatment/foundation.
- IPI requires the applicable NCM/TIPI rate; the engine must not invent a rate.
- PIS-Importação and Cofins-Importação include versioned 2026 rules.
- From 01/04/2026, reduced Cofins benefits subject to the 10% linear adjustment are represented at 0.965% effective (0.97% display), or 1.565% effective (1.57% display) where the additional 0.60 p.p. applies.
- Special regimes require their legal foundation and eligibility; selecting a regime does not automatically zero taxes.

This boundary is a release guardrail, not a claim of exhaustive NCM-by-NCM coverage.
